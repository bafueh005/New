# Boasystemz — Brand & Voice Guidelines

The expanded, human-facing version of `lib/content/brand.ts` (which the AI content engine reads). Keep the two in sync — if you change positioning here, update the constant.

## Positioning

**One line:** Boasystemz helps IT professionals stop endlessly applying and start landing interviews and offers.

**The core belief:** Landing a role is a *strategy game, not a numbers game.* The fix for "no callbacks" is rarely more applications or more certs — it's sharper positioning, an ATS-proof resume, a credible technical narrative, and tested interview reps.

**Why we're credible:** Senior engineers and hiring managers who have been on both sides of the table. We give away real, specific value; the paid programs apply the same frameworks 1:1.

## Audience personas

1. **The Overlooked Engineer (mid-career).** Competent sysadmin/engineer with real skills, 200+ applications, few callbacks. Problem is positioning and resume, not ability. Wants: to be seen. Buys: resume optimization, LinkedIn branding, coaching.
2. **The Specializer (mid-to-senior).** Sysadmin or generalist moving into cloud/DevOps/security. Wants a roadmap and proof projects. Buys: mentorship, career-transformation programs.
3. **The Career-Changer / Breaker-In (early).** Help desk, support, or from another field. Overwhelmed by paths and certs. Wants: an ordered plan. Buys: the toolkit, consultations, foundational programs.

## Value propositions
- Diagnose *why* you're not getting interviews (free 30-min consult).
- ATS-proof resume + outcome-driven bullets that pass screens.
- Interview reps until your answers are boring to deliver.
- A 90-day applying-to-offer plan, not a spray-and-pray.
- Senior, technical mentors who actually hire.

## Tone
- Direct, credible, senior-engineer-to-engineer.
- Specific and technical where it earns trust; plain-spoken otherwise.
- Encouraging, never hype or "hustle-guru." No fake urgency.
- Educational first, pitch second.

## Style rules
- US English. **No em-dashes** in user-facing copy (use commas or rewrite).
- Avoid clichés: "game-changer", "unlock", "supercharge", "leverage" (as a verb).
- No emoji spam. At most one, purposefully, on social.
- Numbers and specifics over adjectives ("6 callbacks in 2 weeks" beats "amazing results").

## Visual identity
- **Palette:** navy `#0f172a` / `#060c1c`, cyan accent `#22d3ee` / `#0891b2`, slate neutrals, emerald for success. (Matches the site's Tailwind theme.)
- **Logo:** "Boa**systemz**" wordmark, cyan on the second half. `app/icon.svg`.
- **Type:** system/sans for UI; clean sans for social graphics. Avoid generic Inter/Roboto for hero graphics — use a distinctive sans.
- **Imagery:** real tools and terminals over stock handshakes; dark UI screenshots; restrained.
